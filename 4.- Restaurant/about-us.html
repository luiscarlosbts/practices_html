About Sapori di Roma Restaurant

Our Story: A Legacy of Flavor and Hospitality

Founded in 1985 by James Roalts, Sapori di Roma Restaurant began as a humble neighborhood eatery with a simple mission: to bring people together over delicious food and heartfelt hospitality.
What started as a small kitchen serving homemade recipes quickly grew into a beloved gathering place for locals and visitors alike. Inspired by the rich traditions of Italian cuisine, our menu celebrates bold flavors and timeless techniques passed down through generations.

Every Dish Tells a Story

From our signature House-Made Lasagna al Forno to our handcrafted Tiramisu, each bite reflects our commitment to quality, tradition, and community.
Over the years, Sapori di Roma Restaurant has evolved with new ideas and fresh interpretations of classic dishes, but our core values remain the same.
We still believe in using fresh, locally sourced ingredients, offering warm and attentive service, and creating a welcoming space where memories are made.

Whether you’re a longtime regular or visiting us for the first time, we invite you to be part of our story and to taste the difference that passion and heritage make.
